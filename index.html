Avoid the costly ones

Six mistakes. All avoidable.

01

Zone chosen on price

Choose by who pays you and where, not by the cheapest licence.

02

Loose licence activities

Activities decide approvals, invoicing and what a bank will accept.

03

Bank account left for last

Prepare the bank file while the licence is being issued.

04

Tax and VAT ignored

Corporate tax is 0% up to AED 375,000 and 9% above. Free zone firms still register and file.

05

Wrong office

Mainland needs a registered tenancy. Free zones often include a flexi-desk.

06

No legal or audit review

Fixing a weak structure after revenue or funding costs far more.
